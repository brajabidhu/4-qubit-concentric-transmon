Suggested manuscript wording:

Three-dimensional electromagnetic simulation of the Q1 flux-bias line. The color scale represents the logarithm of the magnetic-field magnitude |B|, while black arrows indicate the local direction of the real component of the magnetic field. The high-field region is emphasized to resolve the spatial concentration of the magnetic field around the flux-bias structure.

IMPORTANT:
Do not identify the numerical cell attribute as the physical FBL conductor unless the attribute-to-geometry mapping has been independently verified from the original Gmsh/PALACE mesh.
